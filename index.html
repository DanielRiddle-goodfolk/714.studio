02What is Studio 7:14?

Whole-person wellness belongs in one conversation.

Studio 7:14 brings science, nature, hospitality, movement, education, prayer, and biblical truth beneath one roof. Our work begins with the conviction that the body is an integrated design—not a collection of unrelated parts.

We create thoughtful places to support the body’s God-given ability to heal and thrive, while helping each person understand a clear and practical next step.
